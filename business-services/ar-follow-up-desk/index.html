What we do

  • Load and reconcile the aging file
  • Verify invoice and customer contact information
  • Run client-approved reminder cadence
  • Track promise-to-pay and missing-document requests
  • Route disputes/short-pays and report aging/exception state

Good fit when

  • B2B companies with an aging follow-up backlog
  • Controllers who want administrative follow-up without a collection agency
  • Growing firms where billing staff are stretched across too many tasks

What we need from you

  • Aging report and invoice source
  • Customer billing contacts
  • Approved reminder and escalation language
  • Named owner for disputes, concessions and contractual issues

What done means

Every in-scope invoice has a current follow-up state, next action, promise/payment status or named dispute/exception owner.

Boundary

This is AR administration, not third-party debt collection. We do not make legal demands, threaten action, report credit or negotiate disputed legal debt. Payments continue directly to the client.

Common questions

Is this collections?

No. The launch service is ordinary B2B invoice administration and follow-up. Disputed or escalated debt remains with the client or its authorized collection/legal provider.

Do customers pay Good Remedy?

No. Payments continue to the client.

Can you work from our accounting system?

Yes, with authorized access or a defined aging-file handoff.
