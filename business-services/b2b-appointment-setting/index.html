What done means

The customer gets a clean disposition trail from target through reply, qualification, meeting booked, meeting held or disqualified, plus a weekly operating report.

Boundary

We do not invent personalization, customer proof or product claims, and we do not guarantee a number of meetings or sales. No-show and out-of-criteria meetings are not billed as held qualified meetings.

Common questions

Do you cold call?

The launch product is email-based. Cold calling, paid media and automated LinkedIn messaging are outside the base scope.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

The account must fit the agreed ICP, the attendee must be an agreed buyer or legitimate influencer, they must know why the meeting is happening, and the meeting must actually occur.

Who owns the sales call?

The client does. Good Remedy runs the prospecting and scheduling function unless a broader sales engagement is separately agreed.
