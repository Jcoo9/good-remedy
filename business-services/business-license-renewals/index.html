What we do

  • Identify the license, authority, expiration and current renewal requirements
  • Collect required insurance, bond, CE, entity and qualifier support
  • Prepare renewal forms and packet
  • Route required client signatures or attestations
  • File, track deficiencies and record the renewed expiration date

Good fit when

  • Contractors operating across multiple jurisdictions
  • Field-service companies with recurring renewals
  • Operations teams tracking licenses in spreadsheets and calendars

What we need from you

  • License roster and copies/numbers
  • Entity, qualifier and insurance/bond information
  • Current notices and agency requirements
  • Client signatures or professional attestations where required

What done means

A renewal closes only when the filing has a verified disposition and the next expiration/renewal window is recorded.

Boundary

Good Remedy does not make legal eligibility determinations or professional attestations. New applications, reinstatements and complex qualifier changes may require separate scope or specialist review.

Common questions

Do you handle new licenses too?

The launch price is for straightforward renewals. New applications, reinstatements and complex changes can be scoped separately.

Can you track future renewals?

Yes. A portfolio can be converted into a recurring renewal-management engagement after the initial work is formed.

Do you mark up agency fees?

No. Approved government and third-party fees are passed through at actual cost.

Start with the actual work.

Send the backlog, document set, queue or current process. We will confirm whether the straightforward scope fits before anything moves.
