What we do

  • Confirm the abstraction field set and document population
  • Extract parties, dates, renewal and notice terms, commercial fields and other agreed facts
  • Cite the source location for material extracted terms
  • Flag conflicts, missing pages, unclear language and amendment dependencies
  • Deliver a structured register plus review exceptions

Good fit when

  • Operations or finance teams with contract backlogs
  • Companies preparing migrations, renewals or diligence
  • Teams that need a consistent contract register without asking lawyers to re-key business fields

What we need from you

  • Executed agreements and relevant amendments
  • The business fields the client wants abstracted
  • Existing contract register or identifiers where available
  • Named reviewer for ambiguous or legally sensitive terms

What done means

Every admitted agreement has either a completed abstraction tied to the source or a named exception explaining why a field requires client or legal review.

Boundary

Good Remedy does not provide legal advice, determine enforceability, resolve contract ambiguity, negotiate terms or replace counsel. The abstraction records what the source says and flags where interpretation is required.

Common questions

Can you abstract amendments too?

Yes. The amendment relationship must be identified so the final record reflects the document set rather than one isolated PDF.

Do you interpret legal terms?

No. We extract and organize source-grounded information and flag terms that require legal interpretation.

What format do you deliver?

A structured register agreed at intake, plus source references and a review-exception list.
