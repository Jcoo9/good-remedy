What we do

  • Profile record quality and duplicate patterns
  • Normalize client-approved fields
  • Build stale/incomplete and ownership-inconsistency registers
  • Propose merges/deactivations for client approval
  • Return before/after reconciliation and change log

Good fit when

  • RevOps and Sales Ops teams with accumulated CRM debt
  • Companies preparing a CRM migration or automation rollout
  • Small teams that need a one-time cleanup without a consulting project

What we need from you

  • CRM export or authorized access
  • Client definitions for active/stale and ownership rules
  • Source-of-truth fields where conflicts exist
  • Approval owner for merge/deactivation changes

What done means

The client receives an approved cleanup file or applied change set plus a reconciliation showing what changed, what did not and what still needs judgment.

Boundary

No record is silently deleted, merged or reassigned, and no missing contact/company facts are invented.

Common questions

Do you enrich missing data?

Only with a separately approved source/tool and clear cost. The base cleanup does not invent or silently purchase new contact data.

Can you work directly in our CRM?

Yes where authorized, but destructive changes remain gated by client-approved rules.

Should cleanup happen before migration?

Usually, yes. A migration is a good time to stop carrying duplicate and stale records into the destination system.
