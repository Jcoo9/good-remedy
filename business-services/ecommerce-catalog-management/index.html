What we do

  • Review duplicates, variants and broken records
  • Normalize title, category and attribute fields from approved sources
  • Build a missing-field and conflict register
  • Handle approved new-product uploads and field updates
  • Return a source-linked change log and QA report

Good fit when

  • Brands with duplicate or broken listings
  • Manufacturers and distributors publishing to multiple channels
  • Ecommerce teams with a product-data backlog

What we need from you

  • Catalog export
  • Approved source-of-truth product data
  • Channel requirements and templates
  • Client owner for product claims and merchandising choices

What done means

The output is a reconciled catalog with a change log, unresolved-data list and clear maintenance state.

Boundary

We do not invent product specifications, regulatory claims, certifications, ingredients, dimensions or compatibility.

Common questions

Can you work from spreadsheets?

Yes. A spreadsheet export and approved product source material are often enough to begin a cleanup.

Do you write marketing copy?

The base service is catalog operations and structured product data, not creative copywriting.

Can you maintain the catalog after cleanup?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance is a separate monthly option.
