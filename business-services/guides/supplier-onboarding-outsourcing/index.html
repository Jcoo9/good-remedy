Procurement and AP

What should an outsourced supplier onboarding desk handle?

Supplier onboarding is a good outsourced queue when the client has clear requirements. The outside desk can collect the packet, chase missing items and prepare the record; approval and sensitive bank-control decisions should remain with the company.

Published by Good Remedy · Business service guide

Turn requirements into one visible checklist

The supplier should not get five different requests from AP, procurement and risk. Build one approved matrix for the supplier type and use it to drive collection and completeness review.

Keep bank changes under client control

Banking information is both sensitive and consequential. An outsourced onboarding desk can collect through a secure client-approved path, but final bank creation or change should stay inside the company’s control framework.

Make the queue measurable

  • Supplier invited
  • Documents received
  • Missing item named
  • Approval-ready packet
  • Exception owner assigned
  • Aging since last movement

Start with the paperwork, then learn the real exceptions

Good Remedy’s launch service starts at $299 per month for up to 20 new suppliers. Once the queue is running, the client can see which exceptions actually deserve procurement time.

Hand off the work, not the judgment.

If the job is defined enough to operate, Good Remedy can often take the recurring queue or bounded backlog off your team.
