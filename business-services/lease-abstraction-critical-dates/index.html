What we do

  • Extract parties, premises, term and rent schedule
  • Capture options, notice windows and critical dates
  • Extract CAM/opex, tax, insurance and requested business clauses
  • Reconcile amendments to the base lease
  • Return a source-linked abstract and critical-date register

Good fit when

  • Property managers and occupiers with lease backlogs
  • Acquisition teams reviewing multiple leases
  • Real-estate teams that need critical dates out of PDFs and into an operating register

What we need from you

  • Executed lease
  • All amendments and relevant exhibits
  • Agreed abstract field set

What done means

The deliverable is a reconciled abstract plus critical-date register with source page or clause references and named ambiguities.

Boundary

Good Remedy extracts and reconciles what the documents say. We do not provide legal interpretation, and contradictory or ambiguous provisions are flagged rather than guessed.

Common questions

Can you use our existing abstract template?

Yes. We can work to an agreed field set or provide a standard operational abstract.

Do you reconcile amendments?

Yes. The abstract should reflect the current document set, not only the original lease.

Is this legal advice?

No. Ambiguity and conflicts are surfaced for the client or counsel to decide.
