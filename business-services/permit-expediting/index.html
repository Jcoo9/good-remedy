What we do

  • Identify the authority having jurisdiction and current submission requirements
  • Assemble client-supplied plans, forms and supporting documents
  • Submit through authorized channels
  • Track review and route comments/corrections
  • Resubmit approved corrections and track fees/issuance

Good fit when

  • General contractors and developers with repeat permitting work
  • Retail and restaurant rollout teams
  • Field-service companies whose PMs spend too much time on permit administration

What we need from you

  • Ready technical package
  • Project and property information
  • Qualified-professional plans/certifications where required
  • Authorized filing access and client payment process for government fees

What done means

The permit record shows submission, comments, correction state, fees and verified issuance or a named blocking item.

Boundary

Good Remedy does not provide architecture, engineering, code design, legal advice or licensed trade services, and government approval/timing is not guaranteed.

Common questions

Do you create plans?

No. The launch service assumes the required technical package comes from the client’s qualified professionals.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. We manage the administrative path and visibility, but the authority having jurisdiction controls review and issuance.

Can you work multiple jurisdictions?

Yes. Each permit begins with the current rules for the actual jurisdiction.
