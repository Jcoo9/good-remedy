What we do

  • Build solicitation requirement matrix and compliance checklist
  • Inventory source material and required attachments
  • Draft response narrative from client-approved facts
  • Surface missing evidence, answers and forms
  • Run final completeness review and one revision round

Good fit when

  • Professional-services firms without a full proposal team
  • Government contractors with proposal overflow
  • B2B companies facing an occasional high-value RFP

What we need from you

  • Solicitation and amendments
  • Approved company boilerplate/source material
  • Past performance and personnel information the client is authorized to use
  • Pricing and final-representation owner

What done means

The response packet is organized against the solicitation, missing items are explicit, required attachments are accounted for and the client can make the final submission decision.

Boundary

We do not invent past performance, certifications, staff qualifications, pricing, legal representations or references, and we do not guarantee an award.

Common questions

Do you submit the proposal?

The base service returns a submission-ready packet. Submission can be supported where the portal and client authority permit it.

Who owns pricing?

The client. Good Remedy can place approved pricing into the response but does not independently determine commercial terms.

Can you work from an existing content library?

Yes. A clean source library makes the response faster and reduces unsupported claims.

Send the backlog, document set, queue or current process. We will confirm whether the straightforward scope fits before anything moves.
