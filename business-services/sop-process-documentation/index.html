What we need from you

What done means

The procedure is usable by someone who was not in the drafting conversation and names the places where judgment or escalation remains necessary.

Boundary

Good Remedy does not invent legal, clinical, safety, regulatory or technical requirements. Those controls must come from authoritative sources or qualified client experts.

Common questions

Can you start from a screen recording?

Yes. Recordings, notes, forms and existing documents can all be useful source material.

Do you redesign the process too?

The base package documents the current work and surfaces contradictions. Process redesign can be scoped separately.

What makes the SOP different from AI-generated instructions?

The procedure is grounded in admitted source material, names decisions and exceptions, and is reviewed against the actual operating process.
