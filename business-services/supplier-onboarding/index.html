What we do

  • Send supplier intake and document requests
  • Collect tax, insurance, license and other client-required documents
  • Check completeness against the requirement matrix
  • Follow up on missing items
  • Deliver an ERP-ready onboarding packet and aging/status register

Good fit when

  • Procurement teams onboarding recurring suppliers
  • AP teams with vendor-setup backlog
  • Growing companies that need a defined supplier intake process

What we need from you

  • Supplier roster/contact
  • Client-approved onboarding requirements
  • Secure banking-information process where relevant
  • Named approval owner

What done means

Each supplier is either approval-ready, waiting on a named document, held for an exception or closed. The client sees aging and current state.

Boundary

Good Remedy does not approve vendors, independently change bank details or make legal/risk decisions. Banking changes remain client-controlled.

Common questions

Do you approve suppliers?

No. We form the packet and status; the client retains approval authority.

Can you collect banking information?

Only through a secure client-approved process that preserves the client’s control over bank-detail changes.

Can this become ongoing vendor operations?

Yes. The same operating desk can continue after the initial onboarding process is formed.
