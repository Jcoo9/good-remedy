What we do

  • Detect duplicates and near-duplicates
  • Normalize names, addresses and identifiers
  • Build inactive/dormant candidate register
  • Identify missing required fields and documents
  • Return a proposed merge/deactivation file with a source-linked change log

Good fit when

  • Finance teams with years of accumulated vendor records
  • Companies preparing ERP or AP-system changes
  • AP teams seeing duplicate vendors and stale records

What we need from you

  • Vendor master export
  • Client merge/deactivation rules
  • Available tax/document source records
  • Named approval owner for final changes

What done means

The client receives a reconciled cleanup proposal with every merge, deactivation and unresolved issue visible before changes are applied.

Boundary

Good Remedy never changes bank details and does not silently merge, delete or deactivate vendor records.

Common questions

Do you make the changes in our ERP?

The launch service can produce the approved change file. Direct system changes can be added only with explicit scope and authority.

Do you verify bank accounts?

No. Bank-detail verification and changes remain under the client’s secure controls.

Why do this before a migration?

Moving dirty master data simply recreates the same duplicates and stale records in the new system.
