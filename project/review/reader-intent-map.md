# Reader interest, buyer needs and the AI series

The public article series is now **What is the AI actually doing?** It takes five familiar AI questions and a constructive implementation chapter and carries them through what the behavior is, why it happens, how it can be caught or limited, and how useful work can continue.

The six buyer guides remain the direct implementation route. Each article has its own URL, title, description, substantive explanation, primary research citations and related reading. There is no manufactured ranking, fictional expert or repeated sales pitch.

## Source treatment

Selected original user observations and questions were adapted into new article drafts. The private source comparison is held separately for the founder and is excluded from this publishing package. Scientific explanations and proposed controls are new editorial synthesis with primary references, not claims that the founder previously wrote every sentence.

The public series explains principles and ordinary operational checks. It excludes proprietary operating grammar, internal prompts, source hierarchy, implementation algorithms and private context. This is a disclosure screen, not a legal IP opinion.

Consciousness is treated as an interpretive question, not itself a failure mode. No claim about subjective experience is settled by a fluent exchange. Deception studies are identified as controlled experiments, not prevalence estimates for routine use.

No analytics or recurring automation has been installed. AI-search observations separately retain exact query, engine/model, date, session context, mention, citation, position and captured answer.

A click can indicate interest. A commercial conversation establishes the person's actual needs. The end-to-end business horizon remains connected operations, even when a reader arrives through curiosity about AI.

Publication, indexing, ranking, AI citations, traffic and leads remain unverified.
