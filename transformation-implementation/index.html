What implementation-ready means

The workflow has to survive more than the demo.

A working model call is not an operating workflow. The business still needs a current job definition, sources, permissions, handoffs, exception paths, evidence and a way to tell whether the work actually finished.

Good Remedy works at that boundary. The model, agent or implementation platform can change. The customer still needs the work to remain intelligible and transferable.
